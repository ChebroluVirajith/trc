Sarvaagami

SARVAAGAMI is your canvas to design and test your robot’s endurance, might and strategy on man-made and natural terrain, which tests your skills. Starting with designing and building a robot that roves through the curves of the race track might be easy, but making it run on terrain, hurdles and being an early bird is a tough task. So are you up for the challenge?
