Project Expo

INNOVATE! CREATE! DELIVER!
Everything begins with an idea, but it’s not only about ideas, it’s about making ideas happen. ROBOVEDA’25 presents project-expo , a technical event as part of Pradarshan in which the participants prepare their projects and demonstrate to compete with the best. The value of an idea lies in the use of it. So gear up and get ready to bring the best out of you!

Note: Both school students and UG students are allowed to participate, projects from all domains are accepted.
